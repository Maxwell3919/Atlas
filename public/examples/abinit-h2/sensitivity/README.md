# Actual saved ABINIT H2: two bounded sensitivity measurements

These saved NC-LDA H2 records use native9.10.4 (Ubuntu9.10.4-2ubuntu3), a pinned unsupported reproduction route. A:10Ha, B:20Ha; both sole tolvrs1e-12/nstep30. The unchanged H PSP8, H positions ±0.7Bohr,10Bohr periodic box,Gamma,2bands2/0 occupancy,spin1,iscf7 and FFT algorithm112 are bound in the raw input/output. A and B each took10SCF steps. The cached loose-SCF10Ha baseline took6steps using toldfe1e-6/nstep10; it is a saved reference, never rerun here.

From this package in any location, with Python3,PyYAML6.0.1 and Matplotlib available, run `python3 -B postprocess.py`. It reads only package-relative raw files and official cached source, verifies SHA and regenerates all tables/figures. It never runs ABINIT or downloads/installs anything. The portable parser differs from its reviewed saved parser only in the relative location of the official default-source file. Source/token/rounding checks are preserved. Every raw warning block remains in cases/*/outputs/stdout.txt, with adjacent warning tables.

Signed E_A−E_cached = −7.2e-12Ha; signed E_B−E_A = −0.0196283667499Ha, each with propagated intervals in tables/signed-comparisons.csv. Full two-atom Cartesian force differences and conservative per-atom/max norms are alongside. These are measured sensitivities regardless of size/sign. There is no energy-ordering, local-stability or material-accuracy criterion. Two settings are not a cutoff-convergence series. B−A jointly changes cutoff and automatic FFT:30³→45³ and full average NPW1503→4337. Boxcut changes2.10744→2.18561; the box and geometry do not vary.

Native vres2 is mean-removed output-minus-input potential squared and summed across global FFT/MPI points, with no NFFT division or cell-volume multiplication. It has Ha² scaling, not RMS/density/force error. Equal tolvrs at different grids is not equal pointwise error. ETOT energy g22.14/residual es10.3 and marker1p,e10.2 tokens retain half-last-place intervals. Both final residual upper bounds must be strictly below1e-12 and overlap. Outward norm bounds are numerical enclosures, not invented physical significant digits. Raw netforce≤1e-5Ha/Bohr is an interface smoke guard only; no force projection is used.

A and cached baseline each show first-visible densityfloor warning before iteration1:1275points,minimum−0.34E−04 electrons/Bohr³,floor1e-14. B shows zero visible WARNING blocks. Unsupported-version notices remain for both. Later recurrence and final XC pre-floor density remain UNKNOWN for both; zero/one warnings and DEN/GSR are not a no-clipping oracle. No basis/grid/box,individual-force,isolated-molecule/material or experimental accuracy claim follows.

figures/scf-residual.png uses every positive saved A/B residual, a log residual axis and explicit native stopping line. Its adjacent CSV gives literal tokens and rounding intervals. figures/setting-comparisons.png uses two independently scaled linear energy panels including zero, plus unconnected positive force-norm points on a log axis. Its adjacent CSV contains the exact energy values/intervals and outward norm bounds; graphical floating point is a display only. Neither plot supplies a physical tolerance or convergence classifier.
